Canada, through the **Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (CCCS)**, joined **G7 Cybersecurity Working Group** partners in releasing **Preparing for the Post-Quantum Era: A Call to Action**—a coordinated policy signal that **post-quantum migration cannot wait for a definitive quantum computer announcement**.

## Core message

The publication separates **uncertainty about when** a cryptographically relevant quantum computer arrives from **certainty about what to do before then**:

- **Harvest now, decrypt later** — Adversaries can store encrypted traffic and public-key material today for future decryption.  
- **Authentication risk** — Quantum-capable attacks against legacy public-key authentication could enable impersonation, forgery, and trust-path compromise—not only confidentiality breaks.

## Five priority areas

The call to action organizes international cooperation around:

1. **Awareness** of quantum-related cybersecurity risks and PQC benefits  
2. **National strategies** that make standardized PQC algorithms available and deployable  
3. **Research and development** for practical PQC integration  
4. **Public–private partnerships** to share expertise and lessons learned  
5. **Integrating PQC into cybersecurity requirements**—procurement, regulation, and sector guidance  

It recommends a **phased, risk-based** approach: cryptographic asset inventory, critical-system identification, dependency mapping, and explicit transition plans.

## Canadian context

The G7 statement aligns with Canada’s parallel tracks:

- **[ITSM.40.001](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/roadmap-migration-post-quantum-cryptography-government-canada-itsm40001)** — Government of Canada PQC roadmap with departmental plans, annual reporting, and **2031/2035** migration milestones  
- **[SPIN on GC PQC migration](https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/digital-government/policies-standards/spin/migrating-government-canada-post-quantum-cryptography.html)** — Reinforces measurable progress under government security and digital service policies  
- **[ITSP.40.111](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/cryptographic-algorithms-unclassified-protected-protected-b-information-itsp40111)** — Approved **ML-KEM**, **ML-DSA**, and **SLH-DSA** for unclassified and protected information

For Canadian enterprises and multinationals operating in Canada, the G7 publication is a **normative nudge**: customers and regulators increasingly expect PQC readiness evidence in supply-chain and assurance conversations.

## QTL perspective

Quantum Transition Labs is headquartered in Canada’s federal innovation ecosystem; the G7 call reinforces what QTL practices with clients—**start with inventory and long-lived secrets**, align to **NIST/CCCS-approved algorithms**, and treat **blockchain and wallet layers** as first-class migration surfaces because public keys are often already exposed.
