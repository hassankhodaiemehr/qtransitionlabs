## What we do

We focus on practical, implementation-ready quantum security—not just theory.

- **PQC migration strategy:** Inventory, risk mapping, and roadmap for replacing vulnerable algorithms.
- **Crypto modernization:** Design and review of crypto APIs, key management, and protocol upgrades.
- **Secure architecture design:** End-to-end, privacy-by-design and zero-trust aligned architectures.
- **Blockchain & ledger hardening:** Quantum-safe approaches for chains, wallets, and consensus.
- **AI-assisted security analysis:** Using AI to surface crypto and architecture weaknesses faster.

---

## Why quantum security now?

- **Regulatory pressure:** Emerging standards (NIST PQC, ETSI, etc.) are shaping compliance expectations.
- **Harvest-now-decrypt-later risk:** Sensitive data intercepted today may be decrypted by future quantum computers.
- **Long-lived systems:** Critical infrastructure, government, and financial systems must be secure for decades.

We help you move from “we should think about PQC” to “we have a concrete, staged plan.”

## How we work

1. **Discovery & assessment**  
   We map your cryptographic footprint: protocols, libraries, key lifetimes, and data sensitivity.

2. **Risk & impact analysis**  
   We prioritize systems and data based on exposure, lifetime, and business impact.

3. **PQC migration roadmap**  
   We define phased, low-friction migration paths aligned with your architecture and teams.

4. **Design & implementation support**  
   We collaborate with your engineers, architects, and security teams to implement and validate changes.
