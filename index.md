# Quantum-Ready Security for Critical Systems

Quantum Transition Labs (QTL) is a research‑driven security firm specializing in **post‑quantum cryptography (PQC)**, **cryptographic modernization**, and **quantum‑safe architecture design** for high‑assurance environments.

We help enterprises, governments, and critical infrastructure transition from legacy cryptography to **NIST‑approved PQC algorithms**, ensuring long‑term confidentiality, integrity, and operational resilience.

## Why Quantum Security Matters Now

- **Harvest‑Now‑Decrypt‑Later (HNDL)** attacks are already occurring  
- **NIST PQC standards** are finalized and adoption timelines are accelerating  
- **Long‑lived data** (health, financial, government) must remain secure for decades  
- **Regulators** are beginning to mandate quantum‑safe transition plans  

Organizations that begin migration early reduce cost, risk, and operational disruption.
